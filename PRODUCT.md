# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Caregivers researching ADHD and ARFID in children. The homepage should help them find practical parent navigation and concrete questions without requiring a diagnosis first.

## Product Purpose

Provide caregiver-oriented, evidence-aware information and resources about supporting children with ADHD, ARFID, or both. The site is educational and does not replace individualized advice from a child's healthcare team.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site is a public, static Astro website whose Markdown documents are the content source of truth. Existing routes cover concern-led next steps, safety and urgent help, professional roles, preparation for care, school and daily life, and resources. Do not imply diagnosis or individualized treatment, and preserve direct access to safety guidance.

## Brand Commitments

Use locally hosted stock still-life/object photography only where it genuinely helps caregivers navigate or understand the material. Decorative imagery must not depict children, people, faces, hands, body parts, medical procedures, identifying details, logos, or readable text.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains parent-facing educational guides and local abstract still-life WebP images under `public/images/`. Imagery is decorative, not evidence of effectiveness, diagnosis, or clinical endorsement.

## Product Principles

- Make practical parent navigation and concrete questions easy to find.
- Keep safety guidance visible and distinguish it from routine information.
- Preserve the limits of educational content and avoid implying diagnosis or individualized care.
- Keep Markdown as the source of truth and respect the child's dignity and established safe foods.
