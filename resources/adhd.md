# ADHD resources

These resources provide ADHD education, parent support, clinical guidance, and care navigation. They do not diagnose a child or replace an individualized assessment.

## Government and professional information

- [CDC ADHD hub](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/) — U.S. government information on symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, data, and resources.
- [CDC Clinical Care of ADHD in Children](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/hcp/clinical-care/index.html) — clinical-care summary and links to American Academy of Pediatrics guidance.
- [NIMH ADHD information](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd) — federal mental-health information about symptoms, evaluation, treatment, and finding help.
- [AAP ADHD resources](https://www.aap.org/en/patient-care/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd/) — pediatric clinical and family-care resources; some clinical articles may require professional access.

## Parent support and directories

- [CHADD parent resources](https://chadd.org/for-parents/overview/) — parent education, support resources, and ADHD navigation; local availability varies.
- [CHADD Professional Directory](https://chadd.org/professional-directory/) — U.S.-oriented listings; verify credentials, pediatric experience, and current openings.
- [ADDA Professional Directory](https://add.org/professional-directory/) — ADHD professional listings, primarily U.S.-oriented; confirm pediatric scope and licensing.

## Questions to ask

- Does the clinician routinely assess children this age across home and school settings?
- How will sleep, learning, language, anxiety, autism, mood, medication effects, appetite, and feeding concerns be considered?
- What is the treatment goal, how will progress be measured, and who coordinates with school and other clinicians?

**Reviewed:** September 20, 2026. Verify current scope, age range, cost, referral requirements, and availability directly.
