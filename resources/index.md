# Resource hub for families

A short starting point for finding trustworthy information, service-navigation tools, videos, and research links. Inclusion is not an endorsement, certification, or guarantee of treatment quality.

## Before using a resource

Check the **jurisdiction**, age range, cost, referral rules, language access, waitlist, and whether an organization provides education, a directory, clinical care, or crisis support. A provider listing does not prove pediatric experience, ARFID or feeding-disorder expertise, swallowing competence, insurance participation, or current availability.

For immediate physical danger, choking, breathing difficulty, severe dehydration, or acute mental-health danger, use local emergency services. See [red flags and urgent help](../docs/safety/red-flags-and-urgent-help.md).

## Directory maintenance

Links and services change. Review dates do not mean an organization has verified or endorsed this site. Recheck destination, scope, age range, access notes, and crisis information before relying on a resource.

## Sources and methodology

This hub and its linked pages summarize public descriptions from the organizations listed. They separate government, professional, clinical, directory, advocacy, school, insurance, crisis, and research roles. The linked organizations' own pages are the authoritative sources for current service details.
