# Provider-navigation resources

You do not need to know the diagnosis before asking for help. The right first contact depends on the concern, urgency, location, age, insurance, and whether medical, nutrition, mental-health, feeding-skill, swallowing, or school assessment is needed.

## Directories

- [Feeding Matters Pediatric Feeding Specialist Directory](https://www.feedingmatters.org/resources-support/provider-directory/) — U.S.-focused pediatric feeding provider search.
- [ASHA ProFind](https://www.asha.org/findpro) — directory for speech-language pathologists; ask about swallowing and pediatric feeding experience.
- [CHADD Professional Directory](https://chadd.org/professional-directory/) — U.S.-oriented ADHD professional listings; CHADD states listings are not comprehensive or endorsements.
- [ADDA Professional Directory](https://add.org/professional-directory/) — ADHD professional listings; confirm pediatric experience and licensing.
- [NEDA treatment directory](https://map.nationaleatingdisorders.org/) — searchable eating-disorder treatment resources; confirm that a provider treats children and ARFID specifically.

## Provider-fit questions

- Do you routinely work with children or adolescents in this age range?
- What experience do you have with ADHD, ARFID, pediatric feeding disorder, swallowing concerns, anxiety, autism, or other relevant co-occurring conditions?
- Which problems do you assess, and which require referral to another professional?
- Do you assess swallowing safety when relevant, or coordinate with someone who does?
- How do you coordinate with the pediatrician, school, dietitian, therapist, dentist, or other clinicians?
- What are your insurance, referral, waitlist, language, telehealth, transportation, and geographic limitations?
- How do you protect safe foods, hydration, growth, dignity, and the child's perspective?

## Limits of directories

A directory listing does not prove credentials, pediatric experience, ARFID or feeding-disorder expertise, swallowing competence, insurance participation, current openings, or treatment quality. Verify details directly and ask what the provider does not assess.
