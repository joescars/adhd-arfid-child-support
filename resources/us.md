# United States resources

Verify availability, cost, age range, and clinical scope before relying on a service. This list is a starting point, not an endorsement.

## ADHD

- [CDC ADHD hub](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/): government information on symptoms, diagnosis, treatment, data, and resources.
- [CDC Clinical Care of ADHD in Children](https://www.cdc.gov/adhd/hcp/clinical-care/index.html): links to clinical guidance and the American Academy of Pediatrics recommendations.
- [NIMH ADHD information](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder-adhd): federal mental-health information.
- [CHADD / National Resource Center on ADHD](https://chadd.org/national-resource-center-on-adhd/): caregiver and education resources; check current contact details on the site.

## ARFID and eating concerns

- [NEDA ARFID information](https://www.nationaleatingdisorders.org/avoidant-restrictive-food-intake-disorder-arfid/): overview and support links.
- [NEDA treatment directory](https://map.nationaleatingdisorders.org/): searchable eating-disorder treatment resources; confirm that a provider treats children and ARFID specifically.
- [FEAST](https://www.feast-ed.org/): caregiver education and support for eating disorders. Use its information alongside a child's clinical team.

## School and care coordination

- Ask the school about the appropriate evaluation and accommodation process under local and federal rules. A pediatrician, school psychologist, counselor, or special-education coordinator can explain the path in your district.
- Ask clinicians to document who monitors growth, nutrition, medication effects, and feeding progress.

For family navigation while looking for services, see [Family Voices and Family-to-Family Health Information Centers](https://familyvoices.org/affiliates/). A family navigator can help with information and service navigation but does not provide clinical assessment or guarantee access. Verify local services directly.

## Urgent support

- In the United States, call or text **988** for immediate suicide or mental-health crisis support.
- For medical emergencies, call **911** or use local emergency services.
