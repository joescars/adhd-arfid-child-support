# International resources

U.S. laws, insurance programs, crisis numbers, and provider directories generally do not apply elsewhere. Use these as starting points, then confirm local age range, language, referral rules, cost, and service availability.

## Europe

- [ADHD Europe member organizations](https://adhdeurope.eu/our-members/) — country-level ADHD organizations and local navigation; member organizations determine their own scope and services.
- [FEAST global advocacy organizations](https://feast-ed.org/global-advocacy-organizations/) — international eating-disorder advocacy and caregiver organizations; links do not guarantee clinical care or emergency support.

## United Kingdom

- [Beat ARFID information](https://www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk/get-information-and-support/about-eating-disorders/types/arfid/) — UK-oriented ARFID education and support; UK referral pathways may not apply elsewhere.
- [NHS ARFID information](https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/arfid/) — NHS information about ARFID and accessing help in the United Kingdom.
- [ARFID Awareness UK service locator](https://www.arfidawarenessuk.org/service-locator) — UK-oriented service directory; verify provider fit directly.

## Australia and New Zealand

- [AADPA ADHD Professionals Directory](https://aadpa.com.au/adhd-professionals-directory/) — Australia and New Zealand ADHD services; confirm local registration, age range, fees, and referral routes.
- [ADHD NZ Healthcare Directory](https://www.adhd.org.nz/support/healthcare-directory/healthcare-directory) — New Zealand healthcare-navigation resource.

## Child and youth helplines

- [Child Helpline International](https://childhelplineinternational.org/helplines/) — country-based child and youth helpline locator; coverage and services vary.

## Questions to ask locally

- Which service is the appropriate first contact in this country or region?
- What are the referral, insurance, cost, language, and waitlist requirements?
- Does the clinician work with children this age and with ARFID, ADHD, feeding, swallowing, or co-occurring concerns?
- What local crisis or emergency service should a family use?

**Reviewed:** September 20, 2026. International services and policies change; verify details directly with the linked organization.
