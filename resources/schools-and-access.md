# Schools and access resources

School plans, insurance, community health centers, and dental access vary by jurisdiction and individual need. These links help families prepare questions; they do not guarantee eligibility, a particular accommodation, or a local specialist.

## United States school support

- [U.S. Department of Education: IDEA](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/) — federal information about evaluation, special education, related services, and educational participation.
- [Section 504 parent and educator guide](https://www.ed.gov/media/document/parent-and-educator-resource-guide-section-504-public-elementary-and-secondary-schools-2016-21262.pdf) — general information about Section 504; not individualized legal advice.
- [Center for Parent Information and Resources](https://www.parentcenterhub.org/find-your-center/) — finds state and territory Parent Training and Information Centers; services and capacity vary.
- [CADRE state agency and parent-center map](https://cadreworks.org/find-state-agency-parent-center-information) — special-education, early-intervention, and parent-center navigation.

## Health and insurance access

- [HRSA Find a Health Center](https://findahealthcenter.hrsa.gov/) — U.S. community health centers and lower-cost primary-care access.
- [Medicaid and CHIP](https://www.medicaid.gov/chip) — federal and state children's health-coverage information; eligibility and provider networks vary.
- [Medicaid and CHIP state contacts](https://www.medicaid.gov/about-us/where-can-people-get-help-medicaid-chip) — questions about eligibility, renewals, claims, and providers.
- [InsureKidsNow dentist locator](https://www.insurekidsnow.gov/find-a-dentist) — U.S. dentist search for children with Medicaid or CHIP.

## Questions to ask

- Who is the school's first contact for evaluation, health-related needs, feeding, lunch, or accommodations?
- What information can the pediatrician or feeding team share with the school, with appropriate consent?
- Which services, providers, or dental offices accept the family's insurance?
- What are the referral, waitlist, transportation, language, and cost limits?

## Crisis and urgent support

- [988 Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) — U.S. call, text, and chat mental-health crisis support; not emergency medical care.
- For immediate medical danger, choking, breathing difficulty, severe dehydration, or other emergencies, use local emergency services.

**Reviewed:** September 20, 2026. Verify school procedures, coverage, and service availability locally.
