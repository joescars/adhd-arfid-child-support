# Vetted educational videos

These videos are embedded from channels or organizations with a recognizable government, hospital, or professional-resource connection. They are educational starting points—not diagnoses, treatment instructions, or substitutes for a child's care team. Video availability, captions, recommendations, and advertising can change on YouTube. Open the original video if an embed is unavailable.

The embeds use YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Loading an embedded player may still connect your browser to YouTube; use the direct link instead if you prefer not to load an embedded player.

## Watching safely

- A video can explain a concept without establishing that it applies to your child.
- Do not change medication, supplements, nutrition products, diets, feeding plans, or treatment because of a video.
- Stop watching if a child becomes distressed or the content is not age-appropriate.
- Use the site's [red flags and urgent help guide](../docs/safety/red-flags-and-urgent-help.md) for urgent concerns.
