# Feeding and ARFID resources

These resources cover ARFID, pediatric feeding disorder, swallowing, eating-disorder support, and feeding-specialist navigation. They are educational and do not establish a diagnosis or provide a child-specific plan.

## ARFID and eating-disorder education

- [NEDA ARFID information](https://www.nationaleatingdisorders.org/avoidant-restrictive-food-intake-disorder-arfid/) — overview of ARFID presentations, treatment considerations, and help-seeking.
- [FEAST family guides](https://feast-ed.org/feast-family-guides/) — caregiver education and support; peer and educational support is not emergency or individualized medical care.
- [Beat ARFID information](https://www.beateatingdisorders.org.uk/get-information-and-support/about-eating-disorders/types/arfid/) — United Kingdom information; local referral pathways may not apply elsewhere.

## Feeding and swallowing

- [ASHA Pediatric Feeding and Swallowing Practice Portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/clinical-topics/pediatric-feeding-and-swallowing/) — professional guidance on pediatric feeding disorder, swallowing concerns, ARFID distinctions, assessment domains, and team roles.
- [ASHA ProFind](https://www.asha.org/findpro) — directory for locating speech-language pathologists; ask specifically about pediatric feeding, swallowing, age range, ARFID coordination, licensure, insurance, and openings.
- [Feeding Matters](https://www.feedingmatters.org/) — pediatric feeding-disorder education, family resources, advocacy, and connections to feeding-related specialists and programs.
- [Feeding Matters provider directory](https://www.feedingmatters.org/resources-support/provider-directory/) — U.S.-focused provider search; verify ARFID experience, swallowing competence, insurance, age range, and waitlist.
- [Feeding Matters school toolkit](https://www.feedingmatters.org/school-toolkit/) — school-oriented planning and communication tools; does not create legal entitlements.
