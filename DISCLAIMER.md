# Health and safety disclaimer

This repository provides general educational information and links. It does not diagnose ADHD, ARFID, feeding disorders, allergies, nutritional deficiencies, or any other condition. It does not replace individualized advice from a child's healthcare team.

Do not start, stop, or change medication, supplements, tube feeds, elimination diets, exposure plans, or other treatment based only on these documents. Discuss concerns with the child's pediatrician and ask whether referral to an appropriate specialist is warranted.

## Seek prompt medical help when

A child has signs of dehydration, faints, is unusually weak or drowsy, has trouble breathing or swallowing, is rapidly losing weight, cannot keep fluids down, or has another urgent change in health. In an emergency, use local emergency services.

If a child may be at immediate risk of self-harm or suicide in the United States, call or text **988**. Use local crisis or emergency services elsewhere.

## Privacy

Do not commit names, photographs, medical records, school records, appointment details, or other identifying information about a child to this public-by-default code repository. Keep private notes outside the repository or in ignored paths.
