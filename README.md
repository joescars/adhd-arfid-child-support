# ADHD + ARFID Child Support

A caregiver-oriented, evidence-aware collection of research, practical questions, and vetted resources for supporting children who may have ADHD, ARFID (avoidant/restrictive food intake disorder), or both.

This project began as a personal need: finding clearer, calmer, and more trustworthy information for a 9-year-old child. It is not a substitute for a pediatrician, registered dietitian, mental-health clinician, or eating-disorder specialist.

## Guiding principles

1. Prefer primary, governmental, academic, and professional sources.
2. Distinguish established evidence, clinical guidance, lived experience, and open questions.
3. Avoid shame-based language, forced-feeding advice, and promises of quick fixes.
4. Treat nutrition, growth, hydration, safety, and the child's relationship with food as clinical concerns.
5. Make information useful without pretending that a webpage can diagnose a child.
