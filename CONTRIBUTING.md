# Contributing

This repository is for careful, caregiver-friendly documentation—not diagnosis or individualized treatment plans.

## Source standards

- Prefer government health agencies, clinical practice guidelines, systematic reviews, peer-reviewed research, and established professional organizations.
- Record the publication or update date when available.
- Link directly to the source and note what claim it supports.
- Mark uncertainty, disagreement, and gaps instead of smoothing them over.
- Treat personal stories and forums as lived experience, not clinical evidence.
- Do not copy large passages. Summarize in your own words and retain links.

## Writing standards

- Use non-stigmatizing language and avoid describing a child as difficult, manipulative, or attention-seeking.
- Separate observation from interpretation: describe what happened, when, and in what setting.
- Include age range and location when a recommendation is jurisdiction-specific.
- Avoid rigid food rules, coercion, moral language about food, and unsupported supplement claims.
- Include a clinician discussion prompt when a topic could affect safety, growth, medication, or nutrition.

## Research notes

Use `templates/research-note.md` for new findings. Add a short entry to the relevant index only after checking the source and its scope.
