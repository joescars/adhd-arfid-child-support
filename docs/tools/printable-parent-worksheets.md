# Printable parent worksheets

These worksheets are designed to prepare for care, not to diagnose a child or calculate whether a diet is adequate. Print or copy only what is useful. Do not place identifying child information in a public repository.

Record observations, not labels. Avoid calorie scoring or forced intake unless a clinician has given individualized instructions. This approach is consistent with feeding/swallowing guidance, ADHD care that gathers information across settings, and clinician-led evaluation of symptoms such as constipation.[1][2][3]

## Care coordination while waiting

This optional worksheet is an information-organizing aid for caregivers preparing for a care or school conversation. It is not a treatment or monitoring plan. Leave anything blank that is not useful; stop if completing it increases distress or delays care. For worsening symptoms or urgent concerns, contact the care team or use [red flags and urgent help](../safety/red-flags-and-urgent-help.md).
